👩‍🏫 Parent Coaching Guide & Complete 3-PDF Answer Keys

How to guide Sol (Bilingual 1st Grade Midterm Prep):

  • Dual-Language Audio: Notice this app speaks English for Reading slides/worksheets and Spanish for Lectoescritura. Encourage Sol to repeat out loud after tapping 🔊!
  • Fiction vs. Fantasy Clarity: On the Reading Review PDF (Page 1), the title says "Fantasy vs. Nonfiction" while the circle choices say Fiction / Nonfiction. Remind Sol: Fantasy = Fiction (imaginary/pretend) and Nonfiction = real facts.
  • PDF Typo Alert (Page 4 of Español): The teacher's PDF header on Page 4 says "da-de-di-do-du" by mistake, while the worksheet itself is clearly R - r (ra-re-ri-ro-ru) (`regalo, robot, reloj, risa, ratón, raqueta, rey, rueda, rinoceronte, rosa, río, rubí`). This guide corrects that seamlessly!
  • Medial Syllables & Diphthongs: On Page 3, watch out for indio and cadena (the missing syllable is in the middle!) and duende. Also note río takes an accent mark on í.
  • Sílabas Inversas (`al, el, il, ol, ul` & `as, es, is, os, us`): Have Sol pronounce the vowel FIRST before closing the tongue (`al`) or hissing (`as`) — e.g., a-l → al vs l-a → la!
